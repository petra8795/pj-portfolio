01 — My approach

How I got there

Customer flow design

01
  • Collaborated with UX designer: initiated the process by working closely with the UX designer to conceptualize and finalize the customer flow. I sought approvals and made any needed adjustments.

Wireframe & design phase

02
  • Email screen design: after the approval of the email flow, we transitioned to designing individual email screens.
  • Collaboration with development team: we regularly engaged in discussions with the development team to ensure feasibility and alignment with technical requirements.

Development collaboration

03
  • Handover to development: provided finalized designs to the development team, ensuring a clear and detailed handover.
  • Regular check-ins: scheduled regular meetings with the development team to address any queries and provide clarifications. Collaborated to ensure accurate implementation.
  • Addressing issues: acted promptly to resolve any design-implementation discrepancies, collaborated with the development team for quick solutions.

Quality assurance

04
  • Testing phase: after the implementation was complete, I completed a comprehensive testing phase for both website and mobile platforms.
  • Bug reporting: identified and reported any bugs or discrepancies found during testing. Collaborated with the development team to address and rectify issues promptly.
