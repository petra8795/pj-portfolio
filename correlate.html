System email design.

In the CX team I designed Correlate's system emails, worked out their logic with developers, and helped with UI details like icons and key pages. I also tested everything before it went out.

01 — My approach

How I got there

Customer flow design

01
  • Working with the UX designer: we mapped out the customer flow together. I got it approved and made changes where needed.

Wireframe & design phase

02
  • Email design: once the flow was approved, we designed each email.
  • Checking with developers: regular talks with the developers to make sure everything could be built.

Development collaboration

03
  • Handover: final designs handed to developers with clear notes.
  • Check-ins: regular meetings with developers to answer questions and make sure it was built as designed.
  • Fixing issues: when the build didn't match the design, I worked with developers to fix it quickly.

Quality assurance

04
  • Testing: once it was built, I tested everything on web and mobile.
  • Bug reports: I reported the bugs I found and followed up with developers until they were fixed.
