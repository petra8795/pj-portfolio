Waiting room.

The waiting room is the part of the NU app people see after buying a test kit. It walks them through every step, from taking their sample correctly to getting their results, so they always know where things stand. I worked on it through research, design and close work with the rest of the team.

Date
2022 — 2024
Client
The NU
URL
www.thenu.com

*I signed an NDA, so some details are left out here. I'm happy to talk them through on a call.

01 — Challenges

What stood in the way

1

Clarity of process

After buying a kit, people weren't sure what came next, and that made them trust the process less.

2

User engagement

The wait for results is long. People forgot tasks like the health and lifestyle questionnaires, which the app needs to give accurate recommendations.

3

User experience

Getting from purchase to results needed clear guidance at every step, so nobody got lost along the way.

02 — My approach

How I got there

Discovery phase

01
  • Working with the team: workshops and meetings to agree on the business goals and what we were building.
  • Competitor research: looked at other apps to see what's standard and where we could do something different.

Design and development

02
  • UI/UX exploration: tried different screen layouts to find the one that best showed what the app does for people.
  • Prototypes: clickable wireframes to show the direction in presentations to leadership.

Testing and iteration

03
  • User testing: tests before and after launch, with changes based on the feedback.
  • Ongoing updates: kept improving the design and adding features after launch.
03 — Results

What came out of it

People understood the steps better and trusted the process more. They liked the clear guidance and the reminders about important tasks. It didn't fully fix the drop-off during long waits, though, so the team's focus moved to shortening the time from purchase to results.
