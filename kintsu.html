Kintsu website.

I designed the brand identity and website for Kintsu, a DAAS agency. The client wanted a clean, modern website that provides a sense of trust and professionalism, with all the essential details potential subscribers might need. I kept the overall design minimalistic and incorporated pastel colors to elevate the brand and create a fresh, inviting look.
