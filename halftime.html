Halftime with odds.

Turning the one dead spell in a live match into a product moment: a halftime screen where first-half evidence sits on the pitch and every fact points at a second-half bet.

Company
Sportradar
Year
2026
Role
Research, concept, design
Status
Design proposal

Visuals are held back while this is in review. The process is shown as the version ladder below.

01 — The ask

More than tables at the break

The existing halftime flow showed stats: heat map, shot map, player comparison. Product wanted odds in it, second-half markets backed by first-half stats, so people get more than tables during the break. Two constraints carried over: bet placement stays on the bookmaker's side, and the widget is a fixed 16:9 stage.

02 — Research first

What bookmakers and bettors actually want

1

Halftime is a live window

Live betting is where most of the activity is. Keeping someone inside the live product during the break has value before anything is sold.

2

Builders, not single markets

Bookmakers push the bet builder. So three stat-backed facts become three legs and one slip, not three separate markets.

3

Evidence people over-trust

First-half momentum is exactly what bettors overrate. Every evidence card carries its counter-read, and nothing reads as a nudge to bet more.

03 — The convergence

Nine directions to one, v1 → v7.6

v1 — nine directions

Explore
  • Score + line, story cards, contextual odds, studio mode, player props, crowd picks and more, on a responsive board at three widths.

v2 — the builder

Pitch first
  • First-half evidence sits where it happened on the pitch; each chip is a builder leg with its counter-read.

v4 — a state, not a place

Subtract
  • A recording of the real host showed most of the screen duplicated it, so the entry screen, panels and close button went.

v6 — one entry point

Simplify
  • Odds and players became states over the pitch instead of a second route to the same content.

v7.6 — the pitch sets the scene

Current
  • Two-team heat map with shot outcomes and featured combos. The pitch only has to argue, not to be tapped.
04 — Decisions worth explaining

Small calls with big effects

1

Heat colours are tokens

Team colours fail on grass and when kits clash, so two fixed colours carry the heat and identity lives in the legend.

2

Shots as outcomes

Trajectory curves were most of the clutter and said what the dot already said. Markers stayed, curves went.

3

Depth bounded by data

Player cards are fixed at four rows chosen as evidence, and missing data hides a row instead of showing dashes.
