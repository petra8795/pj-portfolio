02 — About me

Petra Jakljevič

Product designer who also builds

I design for moments that happen fast: a live match on a phone screen, a bet that's one goal away, a halftime break that lasts fifteen minutes. Right now I work on live sports apps, from animated matches you can watch on your phone to the betting features on top of them.

I don't stop at the mockup. I explore in Figma, then build a working prototype, so I can see how it moves and feels on an actual screen. Most of my best decisions come from watching the first version not quite work, and fixing it there.

Before that I spent four years on health apps and websites, including an app that made lab results easy to understand for people without a science background. In my free time I make small games, because nothing teaches you more about how things should feel.

03 — How I work

Design first, then build it to feel it

Every project starts with research and Figma. Once I know where I'm heading, I build a working prototype in a separate demo environment, so everyone can see how it really behaves, not just how it looks. That's the way I like to work, not a rule: some projects stay in Figma from first idea to handoff.

  1. 01

    Research Design

    I start with user needs, competitor analysis and the business goals behind the request. Understanding the problem comes before drawing anything.

  2. 02

    Explore Design

    Working in Figma with Claude, I can explore far more directions in the same time. I then refine them until the design fits the product's existing style, the idea behind it and its end goal.

  3. 03

    Prototype Code

    I turn the chosen direction into a working prototype in a dedicated demo environment, with AI handling the code while I own every design decision. Real motion, timing and data reveal problems a static mockup can't.

  4. 04

    Share Design Code

    One prototype, many uses: developers see exactly how it should look and behave, sales can show clients what's new, and stakeholders can follow progress. The goal: developers reuse its components, so nothing is built twice.
