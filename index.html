02 — About me

Petra Jakljevič

Product designer who also builds

I design for moments that happen fast: a live match on a phone screen, a bet that's one goal away, a halftime break that lasts fifteen minutes. Right now I work on live sports apps, from animated matches you can watch on your phone to the betting features on top of them.

I don't stop at the mockup. I design in Figma, then build it in the real product, so I can see how it moves and feels on an actual screen. Most of my best decisions come from watching the first version not quite work, and fixing it there.

Before that I spent four years on health apps and websites, including an app that made lab results easy to understand for people without a science background. In my free time I make small games, because nothing teaches you more about how things should feel.

03 — How I work

Designing with AI as my pair

I use AI every day, mostly Claude Code. It doesn't do the design thinking for me. It lets me turn a design into something that actually works, fast. I vibe-code to try ideas out, and I check everything carefully before it ships.

  1. 01

    Plan Design

    I write down what it should do and what it must never break. The AI reads this before it touches anything.

  2. 02

    Approve Design

    The AI suggests how to build it. I review it like I'd review a design, and nothing gets built until I say yes.

  3. 03

    Build Code

    We build it in small steps, in the real product. I add sliders for sizes, colours and timing, so I can fine-tune the feel live.

  4. 04

    Verify Code

    I test every change in the real product with my own eyes before it's saved. The final call is always mine.
