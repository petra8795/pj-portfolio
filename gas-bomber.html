Gas Bomber.

A phone-first arena party game where the bombs are farts. Drop beans, dodge the drifting gas, last one breathing wins. The wind is the twist: every cloud drifts, so you can get caught by your own fart if you run the wrong way.

01 — The idea

Readable chaos on a phone

Classic arena bombing, but the explosion is only half the danger. Each burst leaves a cloud the wind carries across the board; rocks and crates stop it, so you hide behind them. Rounds last about ninety seconds, with a surprise event at 0:45 and a closing gas snake at 1:20 so every round ends.

02 — Playtesting

Tuned by watching hundreds of rounds

1

No-winner rounds

At first almost half the rounds ended with nobody left standing. Now the last one to faint wins, and that almost never happens.

2

Overpowered matches

Matches (which set all the gas on fire) were deciding most rounds. I made them weaker, so now they help but don't decide.

3

Bots with personality

Easy bots hesitate and panic. Hard bots kick beans at you and light matches the moment you're in gas.

03 — What it taught me

Game feel is interaction design

Every rule had a reason. When gas slid around blocks it felt like it was chasing you, so I stopped it. A fan that changed the wind felt unfair, so it pushes gas away instead. Small calls like these are exactly what I do in my day job.
